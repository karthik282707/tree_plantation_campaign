About the Campaign

The Tree Plantation Campaign encourages individuals, schools, colleges, and communities to plant trees and protect the environment. Together we can reduce pollution, improve air quality, and conserve biodiversity.

Benefits of Planting Trees

Clean Air

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen.

Water Conservation

Tree roots help retain water and reduce soil erosion.

Wildlife

Trees provide shelter and food for birds and animals.

Climate Balance

Planting trees helps reduce global warming.
